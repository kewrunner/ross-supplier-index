Ross Stores, Inc. (NASDAQ: ROST) is the largest off-price apparel and home fashion retailer in the United States, operating over 1,700 Ross Dress for Less and 320+ dd's DISCOUNTS stores across 43 states, DC, and Guam. The company sources name-brand merchandise at 20-60% below department and specialty store prices through opportunistic buying, supported by AI-driven supply chain technology and partner integrations.
